## Notes

- TDT applies only to rentals ≤ 6 months. Longer-term rentals are explicitly exempt from TDT (branch note makes this clear).
- Airbnb/VRBO do NOT remit TDT on the property owner's behalf — critical misconception addressed in both the tcslc page and base tree messaging.
- Routine filing is fully online (TouristExpress); no in-office visit needed unless registering, deactivating, or adding a property manager.
- Military TDT exemptions are mentioned on the tcslc page ("certain members of the military") but without specific statute cite — tree surfaces the fact but points customer at the TDT office for current eligibility rather than inventing a rule.
