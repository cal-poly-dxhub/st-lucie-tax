## Notes

- Authority source for eligibility + exemptions + fee structure is the Florida Fish & Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC). Tax collectors sell these licenses as an authorized agent.
- Exemptions per §379.353: under-16 youth, 65+ residents, disabled residents, armed-forces-home-on-leave, homestead-county exemption.
- Non-resident visitor licenses available in 3-day, 7-day, annual, and 10-day (hunting) durations.
- Military Gold Sportsman's License is specific to Florida-resident active-duty; vets without current active status don't qualify for that particular reduced rate.
- Lifetime licenses are residents-only.

## Authority source

- FWC (myfwc.com/license/recreational/) is the state authority. Fees are published on FWC's site.
